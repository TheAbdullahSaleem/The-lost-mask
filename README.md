**The Lost Mask** is a 2D sandbox action-platformer where survival, resource gathering, and intense boss battles collide. Dig deep into the earth to mine resources, craft powerful upgrades, and step through mystical portals to reclaim the scattered halves of an ancient artifact.

### ✨ A Labor of Love
**Every single sprite, background, and UI element in this game is 100% uniquely hand-drawn by us!** 

---

## 📖 About the Game

You start on the surface with nothing but a basic pickaxe and a vast underground world to explore. Mine dirt, stone, iron, and diamonds to gather materials. Use your gathered resources at the crafting table to upgrade your gear and build bridges across the world.

But you aren't just here to mine. An ancient mask has been split in two and hidden away in dangerous pocket dimensions. You'll need to gear up, unlock the arena doors, and battle two unique bosses:
1. **The Giant Golem** - A massive stone beast that shatters into smaller, faster enemies as you fight it.
2. **The Kid** - A formidable foe guarding the final piece of the artifact.

Reclaim the Left and Right halves of the Lost Mask, combine them in your inventory, and unlock the mysterious cage on the surface to discover the final treasure!

## 🌟 Features

* **Fully Destructible Sandbox:** Mine and place blocks dynamically anywhere in the world.
* **Crafting & Progression:** Gather ores and charcoal to forge Iron and Diamond pickaxes for blazing-fast mining speeds.
* **Boomerang Combat:** Throw your pickaxe to damage enemies from afar—but be careful, you can't throw it again until it returns to you!
* **Boss Arenas:** Seamlessly transition between the overworld and locked boss arenas. Your inventory and world progress are safely preserved while you fight.
* **Custom Audio & SFX:** Responsive audio feedback for mining, walking, jumping, UI interactions, and enemy damage.

## 🛠️ How to Run Locally

If you want to download the source code and run the game yourself:

1. Download and install [Godot Engine 4](https://godotengine.org/download/).
2. Clone or download this repository to your local machine.
3. Open Godot and click **Import** in the Project Manager.
4. Navigate to the folder where you extracted the repository and select the `project.godot` file.
5. Once the project opens in the editor, press `F5` (or click the Play button in the top right) to run the game!
